Coords from CSV

Choose a CSV with columns BH_ID, LAT, and LONG, then a KMZ or KML. Each point placemark must have <Point><coordinates>. Match CSV rows by ExtendedData BH_ID or by <name> (e.g. B6A). The file is checked before any edits. BH_ID matching tolerates common spelling differences (e.g. B6A vs B-6A, B06A, or BH-6A).
